# Startup recovery baseline — 2026-11-09

This checkpoint adds fault reporting and startup retries, patched local TI
mmWave control sources, cooperative SPI/control-task teardown, a finite-capture
boundary guard, and a reproducible `build.bat` for the installed TI toolchain.

**Known remaining failure: only 6 of 10 repeated captures passed.** This commit
preserves the tested version before investigation of the silent stream stall.

The ten trials reset/reloaded the AM273x through CCS, then configured/rearmed
the DCA1000. They alternated CCS attached/detached, used the normal infinite
profile with no injected failures, and did not physically power-cycle boards.

All four failures produced 15296 received payload bytes, then stopped. DCA
configuration and record-start commands succeeded. No CPU fault/assertion was
reported, and no automatic retry occurred. In the run-10 snapshot CSI continued
to progress despite only two CBUFF transfers. The exact cause remains open.

The startup check declares success after two RF frames, before this profile's
five discarded frames plus synchronization frame have completed. It therefore
does not cover the first actual LVDS transfers. The infinite-capture monitor
also does not currently recover a silent stall.

Other tests demonstrated recovery from the original slave CPU fault (type 4,
LR 0x00018800), a one-frame capture emitting exactly 384 transfers / 6291456
bytes with clean shutdown, and one successful 60-second detached recovery run.
These successes do not establish general reliability. Packet gaps were present
near receiver startup; ADC sample/chirp alignment has not been validated.
